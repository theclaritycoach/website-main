# Who to recruit into the CCE Talent Network: a future-of-work brief

Prepared November 1, 2026, for Precious Azurée. Research covers the WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025, PwC's 2026 Global AI Jobs Barometer, Deloitte's 2026 agentic AI research, BCG's 2026 workforce work, LinkedIn's emerging-jobs data, and current certification programs. Sources are listed at the end, with a note on how much weight each deserves.

---

## The short version

1. **The market is moving from "use AI tools" to "redesign work around people and AI agents."** Most organizations haven't done that redesign yet. That gap is CCE's lane, because it needs people work and systems work at the same time.
2. **Three roles are missing from the catalog:** someone who keeps AI agents running after launch, someone who handles AI governance and compliance, and someone who redesigns jobs and workflows for human-AI teams.
3. **Human skills are rising in value, but self-ratings won't show you who has them.** Judge them in the Alignment Call and scorecards, which you already have. The one human skill worth adding to the checklist is reviewing and verifying AI output.
4. **Claude certifications are only open to people at Claude Partner Network organizations, using a company email address.** Your planned "company alias email" onboarding step may be the way in for your talent. Confirm with Anthropic that contractors qualify before you promise it.
5. **Recruit in waves.** Start with the roles that deliver your entry offer and short engagements. Add the technical builders and the recurring-revenue roles next.

## 1. What's changing

### AI skills pay more, and the demand is accelerating

PwC analyzed more than a billion job postings across 27 countries. Its 2026 barometer found that jobs requiring specific AI skills are growing roughly eight times faster than the overall job market (69% versus 9%), and that postings requiring AI skills advertise an average 62% wage premium. The premium varies widely by sector: it was lowest in government and public sector roles (about 16% globally).

**What it means for you:** AI-fluent professionals have options and will expect project rates to match. Your network's offer to them needs more than pay: real client problems, defined pathways, credentials, and a community. That's already in your positioning ("You don't have to arrive fully formed"), and it's a real advantage against firms that only offer billable hours.

### The value is in changing how people work

BCG estimates that about 70% of the value from AI comes from changing how people work, with 20% from technology implementation and 10% from the algorithms. Deloitte's 2026 research found that most companies haven't redesigned jobs around AI (a secondary write-up of its State of AI in the Enterprise survey puts it at 84%). Fewer than half of organizations have defined how humans and agents will work together, even though three-quarters of leaders see more value in collaboration than in pure automation.

**What it means for you:** "Develop People. Build Better Systems." is the right thesis for this market. The talent you need can do both halves, or can partner well with someone who does the other half.

### New work is forming around AI agents

Deloitte describes emerging work in agent process design, agent governance, and agent supervision. It also notes that entry-level staff may need manager-like skills: delegating to agents and reviewing what they produce. LinkedIn data reported by Computerworld shows "forward-deployed engineer" postings grew 42-fold between 2023 and 2025, against 13-fold for AI engineers overall. A forward-deployed engineer sits with the client, builds on their real systems, and stays until it works.

**What it means for you:** your small-business and nonprofit clients won't have anyone in-house to run agents once they're live. Someone has to monitor them, fix them, and update them as the business changes. That's recurring work, and recurring revenue for CCE.

### Regulation is creating governance work, including in Texas

The Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (TRAIGA) took effect January 1, 2026. It applies to Texas state and local government, and it reaches private companies serving Texas residents. Government agencies must tell people when they're interacting with an AI system. Compliance-focused secondary sources say the law offers a safe harbor for organizations that substantially follow the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. The EU AI Act's high-risk obligations begin in August 2026, which matters for any client with European customers.

**What it means for you:** your government and regulated-industry clients (healthcare, insurance, financial services) will need help with AI policy, risk assessment, and disclosure. Today "AI governance and policy" is one skill under AI Transformation Strategist. The demand is growing large enough to justify its own role.

### The core human skills employers want

The WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025 ranks the top core skills as analytical thinking; resilience, flexibility and agility; leadership and social influence; creative thinking; motivation and self-awareness; empathy and active listening; curiosity and lifelong learning; technological literacy; talent management; and service orientation. AI and big data, networks and cybersecurity, and technological literacy are the fastest-growing skills.

**What it means for you:** most of these show up in how someone thinks and works, not on a resume. Your post-call scorecard already covers systems thinking, learning readiness, AI aptitude, communication, and team fit. That's where these skills belong. Asking applicants to rate their own resilience or curiosity adds noise without telling you anything.

## 2. Roles to add

Ranked by how directly each one serves your current clients and offers.

### A. AI Operations Specialist (AI & Automation). Strong recommendation

The person who keeps AI agents and automations healthy after launch: monitoring, fixing breaks, tuning prompts, measuring whether the agent still does the job, and updating workflows as the business changes.

- **Why now:** nobody at a $1M to $10M business is going to run their agents in-house. Without this role, every build CCE delivers slowly degrades after handoff.
- **Business case:** turns one-time builds into monthly retainers.
- **Suggested skills:** agent monitoring and maintenance; AI output quality review and evaluation; prompt and workflow tuning; incident response for AI systems; usage and cost tracking; client support and handoff documentation.
- **Good backgrounds:** automation specialists, IT support and systems administrators, QA testers, operations analysts.

### B. AI Governance & Risk Specialist (AI & Automation, or Strategy & Transformation). Strong recommendation

Writes AI policies, runs risk assessments, maps systems to frameworks (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001), and handles disclosure and documentation requirements.

- **Why now:** TRAIGA is in effect, government clients are in your pipeline, and regulated industries are on your client list.
- **Suggested skills:** AI policy development; AI risk assessment; NIST AI Risk Management Framework; ISO/IEC 42001 AI management systems; AI regulatory requirements (TRAIGA, EU AI Act, sector rules); AI vendor risk review; AI use disclosure and documentation.
- **Good backgrounds:** compliance officers, privacy professionals, internal auditors, risk managers, government policy staff.
- **Certifications:** IAPP AIGP; ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer.

### C. Human-AI Work Design Strategist (People & Workforce). Recommended

Redesigns roles, workflows, and team structures for a mix of people and AI agents. Maps which tasks move to AI, which stay human, and what new skills people need. Builds the reskilling plan.

- **Why now:** this is the 84% of companies that haven't redesigned jobs, and it's the core of the people-plus-systems offer.
- **Alternative:** if you'd rather keep the role list short, add these skills to Workforce & HR Strategist and Change & Adoption Strategist instead.
- **Suggested skills:** task and role analysis for AI; human-AI workflow design; skills taxonomy and skills-based job architecture; reskilling and upskilling program design; workforce impact assessment.
- **Good backgrounds:** organizational development, HR business partners, industrial-organizational psychologists, process improvement leads.

### D. Proposal / Capture Manager (Cross-practice). Worth considering

You're staffing RFP bids. A strong proposal manager raises your win rate across every practice: compliance matrices, storyboarding, pricing coordination, and the final production push.

- **Certifications:** APMP Foundation and Practitioner (Association of Proposal Management Professionals).
- **Note:** this one serves CCE's growth directly more than client delivery. You may want it as an internal role instead of a network role.

### What not to add as a separate role

**Forward-deployed engineer.** It's the fastest-growing title in AI hiring, but for CCE it describes *how* your AI Engineers and Automation Specialists work, not a separate role. Add "client-embedded delivery" as a skill instead.

## 4. Certifications

### The Claude certification finding

Anthropic's official certification FAQ says exams are only available to people at organizations in the Claude Partner Network, and candidates sign in with a company email on the partner's domain. Personal email addresses are rejected. Four exams exist: Associate, Developer, Architect Foundations, and Architect Professional. Certifications are valid for 12 months. The FAQ lists Developer and both Architect exams as counting toward the partner program's eligibility requirements; the Associate exam does not.

What this means for the network:

1. **Your talent can't sit Claude exams on their own.** Their access likely runs through CCE's partner membership and a CCE email address. Your onboarding checklist already has "Company alias email created" before "Anthropic Academy account created," so you may have anticipated this.
2. **Confirm that contractors qualify.** The official pages talk about people "at" partner organizations and don't mention contractors. Email partner-support@anthropic.com before you tell applicants this path exists.
3. **Claude Associate can't be the only baseline option.** Microsoft, Google Cloud, and AWS exams are open to anyone. Keep all four baseline options so nobody is blocked.
4. **Developer and Architect certifications may strengthen CCE's partner standing.** Ask Anthropic what they count toward for your organization. Even so, keep your current rule: never imply CCE pays for them or requires them.
5. **Track expiration dates.** A 12-month validity means certifications lapse quickly. The database already stores expiration dates; a Phase 3 reminder can use them.

Microsoft's AB-730 (AI Business Professional) and AB-731 (AI Transformation Leader) are confirmed as real certifications aimed at business users and leaders. Microsoft announced AB-731 in beta in late 2025; check that it's fully launched.

### Certifications to add

| Certification | Issuer | Tier | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Governance Professional (AIGP) | IAPP | role_recommended | AI Governance & Risk Specialist, AI Transformation Strategist |
| ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer | Various accredited bodies (e.g., PECB) | specialist | AI Governance & Risk Specialist |
| PMI Certified Professional in Managing AI (PMI-CPMAI) | Project Management Institute | role_recommended | Program / Project Manager, AI Transformation Strategist |
| APMP Foundation | APMP | role_recommended | Proposal / Capture Manager (if added) |

All would go in as needing verification, like the rest. A caution: most demand claims for governance certifications come from training vendors, not independent hiring data. AIGP and ISO 42001 are the recognized names in the space, but treat them as "recognized," not "required."

## 5. Recruiting order

You're building brick by brick, so recruit for what you can sell now.

**Wave 1: deliver your entry offer and short engagements**
- **Client Delivery Lead.** Right now you're the delivery bottleneck. One or two strong leads let you run more engagements at once.
- **AI Transformation Strategist** and **Change & Adoption Strategist.** These deliver the AI Readiness Assessment and the adoption work that follows it.
- **Training Facilitator** with AI literacy skills. Workshops are the fastest offer to sell and deliver.
- **Automation & Implementation Specialist.** The highest-volume build work for small businesses.

**Wave 2: bigger builds and recurring revenue**
- **AI Solutions Architect** and **AI Engineer / Developer** for custom builds.
- **AI Operations Specialist** to turn builds into retainers.
- **AI Governance & Risk Specialist** as government and regulated-industry work grows.

**Wave 3: depth**
- **Human-AI Work Design Strategist**, **Leadership Development**, **Learning Experience Design**, **Executive Coaching**, **Data & Analytics**, and **Proposal / Capture** as your pipeline calls for them.

The network should stay open to every role from day one. The waves are about where you put your outreach effort.

## 6. What this means for the platform

Small changes worth making to the build:

1. **Add the new roles, skills, and certifications to `catalog.yaml`** once you've decided (section 7).
2. **Add a "verified by CCE" flag on skills,** set by an admin after someone has shown the skill on a real project. Skills-based hiring depends on evidence more than on self-ratings. The resume evidence line the AI extracts is a start; project-verified skills are stronger. This is a one-column change, best added in Phase 2.
3. **Show certification expiration** on profiles and make it filterable. The data is already stored.
4. **Keep human skills in the scorecards,** not on the application.
